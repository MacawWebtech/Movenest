About Us

OUR STORY

We started MoveNest after one too many bad moving days

A decade ago, our founder watched a mover toss a box marked "fragile" onto a truck bed. That box held ten years of photo albums. MoveNest exists so that never happens on our watch.

Where we start

Our mission

Make relocation predictable — clear pricing, trained crews, and a coordinator who answers the phone — so moving day is the calmest part of moving.

Where we're headed

Our vision

To be the relocation partner households trust by default — the way you'd trust a friend with a truck, at the scale of a national company.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

The values behind every move

Care

Transparency

Reliability

Accountability
