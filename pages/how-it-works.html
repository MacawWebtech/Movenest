How it works

Six steps, one coordinator, no guesswork

Here's exactly what happens after you request a quote — and what we need from you at each stage.

Step 1

Tell us about your move

Share your pickup and delivery address, home size and preferred date — online or on a call.

What we doYour coordinator reviews the details the same day.
Your partFill in the quote form or call +91 44 6789 0123.
Step 2

Approve your quote & book

You get an itemised, fixed price. Approve it, pick a date and time slot, and your booking ID is issued instantly.

What we doLocal quotes arrive within minutes.
Your partMention fragile, bulky or high-value items.
Step 3

We pack every room

A trained crew arrives with cartons, bubble wrap and blankets, and packs and labels room by room.

What we doEvery carton is listed on a digital inventory.
Your partSet aside anything you want to carry yourself.
Step 4

Loading & transport

Furniture is dismantled, wrapped and loaded. Your truck leaves with live GPS tracking switched on.

What we doTrack the truck from your phone at any time.
Your partDo a final walkthrough with the crew.
Step 5

Delivery at your new home

Your belongings arrive within the agreed window and are carried to the rooms you choose.

What we doItems are checked off the inventory as they come in.
Your partTell the crew where each room’s cartons go.
Step 6

Unpack & settle in

Furniture is reassembled, cartons unpacked on request and packing waste taken away.

What we doYour coordinator follows up within 48 hours.
Your partSign off, rate the crew and flag anything for our protection.
