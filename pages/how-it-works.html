How It Works

HOW IT WORKS

Seven stages, one coordinator, zero guesswork

Here's exactly what happens after you request a quote — and what we need from you at each stage.

01

Tell us about your move

Share your pickup and delivery address, home size, and preferred date.

Expected actionWe use these to prepare an accurate quote.
Your partFill in the quote form or call our team.
02

Get your estimate

We send an itemised estimate covering packing, transport, and handling.

Expected actionThe estimate is ready within minutes for local moves.
Your partReview the breakdown and ask about anything unclear.
03

Confirm your booking

Approve the quote and choose your moving date and time slot.

Expected actionYour booking ID is generated immediately.
Your partShare any special instructions for fragile or bulky items.
04

Packing crew assigned

A trained crew and move coordinator are assigned to your booking.

Expected actionYou receive their contact details in advance.
Your partSet aside items you're packing yourself, if any.
05

Pickup & transport

The crew arrives, packs remaining items, and loads the truck.

Expected actionLoading is inventoried so nothing is missed.
Your partDo a final walkthrough with the crew before they leave.
06

Delivery

Your belongings arrive at the new address within the agreed window.

Expected actionFurniture is placed where you want it.
Your partCheck the inventory list as items are unloaded.
07

Move completed

Sign off on delivery and receive your final invoice.

Expected actionYour coordinator follows up within 48 hours.
Your partFlag anything for our damage-protection process, if needed.
